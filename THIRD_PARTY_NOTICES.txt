Bundled third-party content
---------------------------

  Generic bust (`fieldworkbench/assets/generic_bust_v1.stl` and `generic_bust_v1.regions.json`)

  Derived from the Blender Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 asset bundle, specifically
  the `Body Female - Realistic` base mesh, by Blender Studio and community
  contributors. The Human Base Meshes bundle is distributed under CC0 1.0
  Universal (public-domain dedication). Attribution is not required by CC0,
  but this notice is retained to document the asset's source and provenance.

  Field Workbench derivation: the source body was cropped to a head/neck/upper-
  torso bust, cut surfaces were closed, facial/eye volumes were consolidated,
  the result was voxel-remeshed and decimated, and the final mesh was checked
  as watertight/manifold for engineering visualization, exclusion, and
  measurement use. Named scalp/face/ear/neck-shoulder surface regions were then
  authored on the same fixed topology in Blender and exported as the companion
  `generic_bust_v1.regions.json` face-index map. The mesh is not a patient-
  specific anatomical model or a clinical anatomical atlas.

  Source: https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/
  Bundle archive: https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal

  SRI24 brain and anatomical labels

  Bundled paths:
    `fieldworkbench/assets/sri24_brain_gmwm_v1.stl`
    `fieldworkbench/assets/sri24_labels/`

  Derived from the SRI24 v2.0 multichannel atlas of normal adult human brain
  anatomy by Torsten Rohlfing, Natalie M. Zahr, Edith V. Sullivan, and Adolf
  Pfefferbaum. SRI24 is an MRI population atlas generated from 24 normal
  control subjects and distributed by the SRI24 project under Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0). This derived brain mesh is
  distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms; it is third-party content and
  is not relicensed under Field Workbench's GPL license.

  Field Workbench derivation: the SRI24 v2.0 tissue segmentation was loaded in
  3D Slicer; gray-matter and white-matter labels were unioned while CSF was
  excluded; a closed surface was exported in SRI24 RAS millimetre coordinates;
  the mesh was remeshed/cleaned and decimated in Blender; disconnected remnants
  were removed; and the final single connected surface was verified as
  watertight/manifold. Field Workbench preserves that atlas-space source asset
  and applies a documented rigid NAS/LPA/RPA transform when the built-in brain
  is inserted.

  The official SRI24 v2.0 NIfTI label archive is also bundled for offline
  anatomical-region analysis. It contains the AAL-derived `tzo116plus` and
  LPBA40 parcellations, their upstream label-name/colour tables, the supplied
  supratentorial support mask, and the atlas license. The three original `.nii`
  files were losslessly gzip-compressed with deterministic gzip headers;
  decompression reproduces the source bytes exactly. No voxel values, affines,
  or label tables were altered. File hashes and validation expectations are
  recorded in `fieldworkbench/assets/sri24_labels/manifest.json`.

  The upstream `SRI24-tzo116plus.txt` table omits definitions for nine
  spatially encoded ventricular values that occur in `tzo116plus.nii`. Field
  Workbench preserves that official table unchanged and supplies deterministic
  names/colours for those nine values at load time using the adjacent upstream
  encoding sequence. The affected IDs and policy are disclosed in the asset
  manifest; the LPBA40 volume and table have complete one-to-one coverage.

  The bundled lpba40_meshes.npz display cache is derived from the unchanged
  LPBA40 label volume at a two-voxel visual stride in Field Workbench canonical
  head coordinates. It contains 56 region shells generated with VTK
  SurfaceNets3D 9.6.2. VTK (BSD-3-Clause) is a developer-only regeneration
  dependency listed in requirements-mesh-build.txt; it is not imported by the
  application or distributed in normal Field Workbench binary releases.

  Original label archive: `sri24_labels_nifti.zip`
  Original archive SHA-256:
    ed47fde305baefb687a38e0daa8527896480e4e0159a0a0f56c8cbe09fc97e1f
  Archive download:
    https://www.nitrc.org/frs/download.php/4508/sri24_labels_nifti.zip

  The reduced documentation images
  fieldworkbench/assets/help/sri24_fiducials_1.jpg through
  sri24_fiducials_4.jpg are Field Workbench registration screenshots produced
  in 3D Slicer from SRI24 spgr_unstrip.nii. They document the NAS/LPA/RPA
  fiducial placements and are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms.

  Requested citation:

Bundled Windows video encoder
-----------------------------

  FFmpeg n8.1.2 / x264 / zlib

  The source archive and Windows binary distribution include a small static
  ffmpeg.exe used only
  to convert Field Workbench's numbered PNG export frames to H.264/yuv420p MP4.
  Linux distributions do not bundle this executable and continue to use the
  system FFmpeg installation.

  The bundled executable was built from unmodified FFmpeg tag n8.1.2 with
  --enable-gpl, --enable-version3, --enable-libx264, and --enable-zlib. It
  reports x264 core 165 r3222 b35605a and zlib 1.3.2. The exact configure
  command, executable SHA-256, source locations, and license texts are kept in
  third_party/ffmpeg in the source release and copied to
  licenses/FFmpeg-n8.1.2-x264 in the Windows binary release.

  FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/
  FFmpeg source tag: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n8.1.2
  x264: https://www.videolan.org/developers/x264.html
  x264 source: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264
  zlib: https://zlib.net/

  This is third-party software and is not relicensed as Field Workbench code.


Network-fetched research data
-----------------------------

  UPENN-GBM tumor segmentations

  Field Workbench can, at the user's request, download one de-identified,
  atlas-normalized NIfTI tumor segmentation from the UPENN-GBM collection and
  convert selected label regions into scene meshes. These case files are not
  bundled with the application or source archive. The built-in catalogue covers
  the 147 original expert/curated segmentation assets exposed by the per-file
  data-nih/tcia redistribution.

  Collection citation:
    S. Bakas et al., "The University of Pennsylvania glioblastoma (UPenn-GBM)
    cohort: advanced MRI, clinical, genomics, & radiomics," Scientific Data 9,
    453 (2022). DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01560-7

  Collection DOI: https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.709X-DN49
  Collection page: https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/upenn-gbm/
  Per-file redistribution: https://github.com/data-nih/tcia/releases/tag/upenn-gbm
  Collection license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
  License URI: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  The imported geometry remains research data under the collection's terms. It
  is atlas-normalized generic-head geometry, not patient-specific scalp anatomy,
  and is not intended for diagnosis or treatment planning.

Binary-release notice generation
--------------------------------

build_windows.bat runs tools/collect_release_licenses.py after PyInstaller. The
collector reads PyInstaller's actual Analysis table, maps bundled Python modules
back to the installed distributions in the exact release virtual environment,
and copies only their license/notice/SBOM material into the onedir release. It
then writes the exact distribution and Qt-module inventory below this template
in the binary copy of THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

Qt WebEngine uses Chromium and has additional third-party terms. Keep the
generated licenses/ directory, Qt WebEngine resources, and this notice beside
FieldWorkbench.exe. Relevant official licensing information:

  https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html
  https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html
  https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtwebengine-licensing.html

PyInstaller is used to create the release but is not imported by or shipped as
an application runtime package. Its bootloader has an exception permitting the
generated bundle to use the application's chosen license.
